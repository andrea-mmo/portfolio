About

Hi, I'm Andrea.

I've seen e‑commerce from both sides of the counter. I started in fashion retail, managing a Pull&Bear store for Inditex: leading a team, working to sales targets, planning visual merchandising and learning first-hand what makes people buy, hesitate or walk away.

Then I moved to the other side of the screen. For three years I worked as a front-end developer on a sustainable fashion e‑commerce platform for brands across Europe, where I built the marketing website and the customer dashboard from scratch.

Now I'm focusing my career on digital marketing and e‑commerce, where those two worlds meet: I understand the customer, I know how online stores work underneath, and I can design and build what I plan. I'm from Spain, based in Manchester, and work in English and Spanish.
