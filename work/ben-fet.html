The starting point

This project is at its very beginning. Before asking for content or going into detail, I prepared six directions so the first conversation starts from something concrete.

Asking “what style do you like?” rarely gets a useful answer. Showing real pages to react to does.

The approach

Each concept is a different positioning, not just a different colour palette. It changes who the page talks to, what it asks the visitor to do and which sections it needs to get there.

All six are built as working, responsive pages and gathered on one presentation page that explains, in plain language, what each one conveys, who it suits and what it includes.

  • A decision the client can make alone

    The presentation page avoids design jargon. Every card answers the same three questions, so the options can be compared side by side without me in the room.

  • Seen the way customers will see it

    Each proposal opens in a phone preview from the desktop, and directly in its mobile version when the link is opened on a phone.

  • Open to mixing

    The concepts share the same building blocks, so the style of one can be combined with the sections of another.

  • What comes next

    Once a direction is chosen, I'll replace the sample copy, prices and photos with the company's own and prepare the site for launch.
